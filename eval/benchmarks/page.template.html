Next steps

In order of what the numbers say is costing the most.

Find more moments in the scan done

Every window stopped on its 3-round cap, never on “is there a moment here” — that stayed at 0.88 by round 3. At 6 rounds, moments never anchored fell from 24 to 13 (labeler A) and recall rose from 0.38 to 0.54, for 2–4 points of precision and about 2.5× the requests — trimmed back by stopping the ending search at the first ending that works.

Compare against simpler systems (013) done

jevcut beats all five baselines on recall, clips with both edges right, precision and typical start error — on the 8-video pilot and again on the full 38-video set, including the 30 videos it was never developed on. It does not yet pass the issue’s ship bar: its worst starts are further off than simple padding’s, because when the opening pick misses, it misses by a lot.

Check the chosen opening against the finished clip done

If the opening Choice’s pick reads mid-thought or has no hook once the clip is finished, its next two picks are tried against the same ending. More clips with both edges right on both labelers (35→38, 33→37), recall up, for ~4% more requests. The worst-case tail (below) does not move: most of those misses were never offered as a candidate in the first place.

Judge ads with the surrounding transcript done

A real run shipped sponsor-read pieces cut from the middle of a segment, without the “brought to you by” or the link. The ad check now reads the minute before and after the clip and drops at 0.35: mid-read pieces caught go from 50% to 82%, with none of 545 real clips flagged.

Make Lemonfox the default when a key is set

It raised recall on all four label sets and has the most fully right clips on three of them, for about $0.17 per hour of video. Fall back to local Whisper when there is no key.

Win back precision with a screen question

Lemonfox ships more clips, and precision fell 3–6 points. After ads, the hard negatives the gate still lets through are mostly narration of what is on screen — chart numbers, live demos, deal screens. One “does the point depend on the screen?” question, spread-tested on the labeled texts before wiring in, as the ad check was.

Fix the openings’ worst misses

At the 90th percentile the opening Choice’s starts are still 17–20s off on 38 videos — worse than simple padding — mostly because the right start was never offered as a candidate: the scan’s anchor sits in the thought before the moment actually begins. Two rewordings of the opening question were tried and didn’t move it (RESEARCH.md). This is what stands between jevcut and the baseline gate.

One human review pass

Every labeler so far is the same model, so the 0.0s start agreement is a lower bound. A human pass on two videos would bound the ruler from the other side.

An edge counts as right within 0.3s of the labeler’s range: two transcripts time the same word up to ~0.26s apart. “Found” is a one-to-one match at overlap (IoU) above 0.5. Chance is recall from random clips of the same lengths.
